Website privacy

Updated 3 November 2026. This page covers the TubTime website. The app’s family records stay on your device, as explained in the app privacy policy.

Your choice comes first

Optional Google Analytics helps Matthew Coxall understand which free links bring people here and which pages lead to Google Play. It stays off unless you choose Allow analytics. You can change your choice using Website analytics choices at the bottom of any measured page. Rejecting it does not affect the website or app.

What is measured

After consent, Google receives page views, clicks out to the TubTime Google Play listing, the link’s position, recognised campaign labels and a limited referring-site category. It also processes technical information such as browser/device details and network requests. Analytics cookies provide Google’s pseudonymous browser identifiers. This is not a count of known people, and is not fully anonymous tracking.

We do not send names, email addresses, child profiles, bath records, handwritten printable entries, searches, arbitrary URL query values or full referring-page addresses. Unknown referring sites are grouped as other referrals. Google Play clicks do not prove an installation, Plus purchase or sale.

Cookies, storage and Google

The website remembers your analytics choice in local browser storage. If you allow measurement, Google Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 30 days without renewal on each visit. Google Signals, advertising storage, ad personalisation, user-provided data collection, granular location/device collection and optional account data sharing are off. Event and user data retention is two months, with reset on new activity off; standard aggregate reports may last longer.

Google processes analytics information as our service provider, including possible international processing. See Google’s privacy policy, how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google’s analytics data safeguards. Hosting providers also process normal web requests to deliver these pages.

Turning it off

Choose No thanks through Website analytics choices. We stop our measurement events and remove the Google Analytics cookies this site can clear. This does not erase data already collected by Google. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals keep our analytics off. Blockers and refused consent mean reports will be incomplete.
